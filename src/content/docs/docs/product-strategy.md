## Value proposition

**Hearthline makes the idea of home protection easier to understand, with an established identity, approachable language, and a clear next step.**

The brand proposition is a design hypothesis. Trust and customer preference must be tested with people, and a real insurer would need to substantiate its product and service claims.

## Goals for this version

| Goal | Evidence to seek |
| --- | --- |
| Communicate who the product is for | Visitors identify homeowners as the intended audience |
| Explain the cover categories | Visitors distinguish the home, belongings, and life interruptions |
| Make the next step discoverable | Visitors can find and explain the quote action |
| Demonstrate product judgement | Reviewers can find the rationale, priorities, tradeoffs, and validation plan |

## Prioritisation

**Must have:** a complete landing-page narrative, a recognisable brand, readable mobile layouts, a quote action with an honest endpoint, accessible navigation and FAQs, and an editable case study.

**Next, after validation:** clearer product detail where participants need it, evidence-based changes to messaging, and a separate quote-journey prototype if the value proposition is understood.

**Deferred:** premium calculations, personal-data collection, accounts, payments, policy administration, claims handling, and analytics integrations. These require product, operational, and market decisions beyond this landing-page concept.

## Tradeoffs

| Choice | Benefit | Cost or limitation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Landing page before a quote flow | Keeps effort focused on communicating the proposition | Cannot validate quote completion or purchasing behaviour |
| Explanatory dialog after the quote button | Provides a working interaction and makes the portfolio boundary explicit | Clicking signals curiosity, rather than a completed quote or sale |
| Market-neutral language | Supports the initial presentation without choosing a jurisdiction | Cannot communicate a final, locally defined insurance offer |
| Established visual identity | Gives the concept a coherent and reassuring presentation | Brand credibility remains a hypothesis |
| Separate product and documentation layouts | Serves homeowners and portfolio reviewers with different reading needs | Requires clear links between the two experiences |
| Static Astro implementation | Keeps the presentation lightweight and straightforward to maintain | Dynamic insurance services would require a later architecture decision |

## Decisions that need further evidence

No detailed market-size estimate, pricing model, or acquisition forecast is included because the project has no verified inputs for them. The next product decision is whether this proposition and explanation are useful enough to justify exploring a quote flow.

If visitors understand the categories but hesitate to continue, investigate the information they are missing before adding more promotional copy. If the categories themselves are confusing, revise the language and structure before expanding the journey.
