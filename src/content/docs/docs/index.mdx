**Hearthline is a homeowners insurance landing-page concept.** This case study explains the choices behind the experience: the problem it aims to address, the audience it is designed for, what was prioritised, and how the approach could be validated.

> **Portfolio draft:** The audience, needs, and expected benefits below are working hypotheses. No customer research, conversion experiment, or insurance product launch has been completed. This documentation is an editable starting point for the project owner.

## The brief

Create an established, reassuring insurance brand that helps homeowners understand a proposed product and decide whether to explore a quote. Keep a separate documentation area so a reviewer can inspect the product thinking behind the interface.

| Decision | Direction |
| --- | --- |
| Primary audience | Homeowners exploring protection for their home and belongings |
| Brand | Hearthline, a fictional insurer |
| Market | English-language, market-neutral concept |
| Primary visitor action | Select “Get a quote” to express interest |
| Prototype depth | Landing page, FAQs, navigation, and an explanatory quote dialog |
| Portfolio audience | Hiring managers, product peers, and other reviewers |

## The product hypothesis

**If a homeowner can quickly understand what protection relates to, see the proposed next steps, and resolve common questions, they may feel more confident exploring a quote.**

The landing page tests the communication of that proposition. It cannot establish pricing appeal, policy suitability, quote completion, or purchase intent on its own.

## What is implemented

- A responsive product landing page with a consistent visual identity.
- Three illustrative cover categories: the home, belongings, and interruptions to everyday life.
- A proposed three-step customer journey, clearly described as the full-product vision.
- Expandable FAQs and a quote button that explains the prototype boundary.
- Keyboard-accessible navigation, a modal dialog, reduced-motion support, and locally served imagery and fonts.
- This documentation area, including searchable decision records and an initial validation plan.

The prototype does not collect personal details, calculate premiums, issue policies, handle claims, or send analytics events.
